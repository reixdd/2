---
name: colosseum-gameplay-design
description: Preserve COLOSSEUM's model-first RPG presentation and accessible interaction loop.
---

Before gameplay changes inspect the current registry, shared build state, battle events, and approved artwork. Keep model identity prominent and fantasy identity secondary. Browsing a fighter must not switch the active build; require confirmation. Build equipment modifies exact instruction text, never fictional intelligence stats. Use three reachable equipment sockets, with explicit legacy support for older four-item saves.

The loop is select → equip → save → challenge → checker → evidence → journal. DOM/CSS battle standees are acceptable; keep outcomes independent from animation. Offer keyboard, touch, skip, reduced motion, low effects, and clear failure/cancellation paths. Keep the equipment dock reachable at 1366×768 and 390×844. Do not invent skill dependencies, player activity, urgency, or damage. Test the whole loop before exporting.
